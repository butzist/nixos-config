Stylix themes built from one settings attrset per theme. mk-theme.nix emits
.system and .user variants; callers select them explicitly at the import site.

mk-theme.nix             Theme builder: stylix settings shared, gtk only for .user.
bosque-kingdoms.nix      Bosque dark, everforest-2.png, Banana cursor, FiraCode Nerd Font.
                         Imported by nixos-modules/base.nix as .system and by adam/work as .user.
ubuntu-catppuccin.nix    Catppuccin Frappe dark, ubuntu-catppuccin.png, same cursor/fonts.
                         Imported by games/mirj as .user.
