tack-managed sources: declarative pins and resolved lock.

default.nix       Tack resolver; treat as generated and let tack update it.
pins.toml         Declared pins, follows and patches.
pins.lock.json    Resolved revisions; this is the lock file to commit.
patches/          Vendored pin patches; add with `tack patch add`.

Tack also writes .tack/diffs/ when a pin has patches; do not hand-edit it.
